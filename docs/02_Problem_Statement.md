# Chapter 2: Problem Statement

## 2.1 The Urban Parking Crisis
Rapid urbanization and increasing private vehicle ownership have created significant parking management challenges in metropolitan centers, shopping malls, hospitals, and corporate parks.

## 2.2 Operational Vulnerabilities in Conventional Systems
1. **Inefficient Manual Ticketing:** Manual gate security checks require operators to visually inspect plates and handwrite or print thermal paper tokens. This introduces bottleneck delays, leading to severe traffic spillover onto public thoroughfares.
2. **Hardware Fragility & Excessive Capital Expenditure:** Conventional automated lots rely on physical ground sensors (ultrasonic, geomagnetic, or infrared) installed per individual bay, alongside RFID windshield tags. These hardware components are vulnerable to weather wear, water damage, and mechanical stress, resulting in high installation and maintenance overheads.
3. **Revenue Leakage & Lack of Audit Trails:** Handwritten logs and unmonitored exits frequently suffer from billing calculation mistakes, untracked overstays, and lost paper tickets.
4. **Security Vulnerabilities:** Facilities lack automated means to screen for stolen, flagged, or trespassing vehicles entering the premises in real time.
